# DEADZONE

## SURVIVE THE FOG. HOLD THE HOUSE.

**Deadzone** is a first-person zombie survival game with base defense elements, created as a university project for **UNINTER Digital Games**.

You are trapped in a contaminated world where a single house is your last safe refuge. Between waves, you prepare your defenses, manage resources, and decide how much risk to take outside in the toxic Deadzone. Then the horde comes.

## PROJECT STATUS

Deadzone is currently in **active development**.

The game is playable in concept and focused on building and validating its core experience: wave survival, combat, progression, and strategic preparation between attacks.

## GAME THEME

The world is covered by a deadly toxic fog known as the **Deadzone**.

Zombies roam outside, and survival depends on smart choices: fight efficiently, invest your points, and defend your safe house against increasingly dangerous waves.

## GAMEPLAY OVERVIEW

Deadzone runs on a clear two-phase loop:

- **Preparation Phase:** buy gear, refill ammo, plan your defense, and get ready.
- **Combat Phase:** trigger the horde and survive until every enemy in the wave is eliminated.

Step by step, waves become harder, pressure increases, and every decision matters.

## HOW TO PLAY

Your goal is simple: **survive as many waves as possible**.

Earn points by defeating enemies and use them to strengthen your build, improve your weapons, and stay alive longer.

### CONTROLS (BASED ON GDD)

- **Move:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`
- **Look:** Mouse
- **Sprint:** `Shift`
- **Jump:** `Space`
- **Shoot:** Left Mouse Button
- **Aim:** Right Mouse Button
- **Reload:** `R`
- **Switch Weapon:** `1`, `2`, `3`...
- **Interact / Use:** `E`
